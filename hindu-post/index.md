# Hindu Post - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-18

Hindu Post ("the app") is published by Vivek Kumar ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles. Contact: vivekforwork1@gmail.com

## Summary
- No account, no login.
- Your favorites, created quote images and settings stay on your device.
- We do not sell your data.
- Ads are shown through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers for advertising, subject to your consent choice.

## Data stored on your device
Favorites, your created quote images, language, text size, reminder time and purchase status are stored locally on your device. We cannot access this data. You can delete it at any time with Settings > Delete my data, or by uninstalling the app.

## Data collected by third parties
- **Google AdMob (ads):** may collect the Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. In regions where consent is required you are asked through Google's consent form (UMP) before ads load. You can change your choice in the app and in your Android ads settings (Settings > Google > Ads). We do not use religion-based or other sensitive-interest targeting. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google Play Billing (optional purchase to remove ads):** payments are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

## Permissions
- Notifications: only to show the daily quote reminder you choose. You can turn it off in Settings.
- Photo Picker / gallery: only when you choose a background image. The app does not read your photo library on its own.
- Internet: only for ads and Google Play Billing.
The app does not access your contacts, location, microphone, camera or messages. Sharing to WhatsApp or other apps happens only when you tap Share and pick the app yourself.

## Children
The app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

## Data retention and deletion
We do not hold your personal data on any server. Local data stays until you delete it (Settings > Delete my data) or uninstall the app.

## Content
Quotes are from public-domain scriptural and traditional sources with original short meanings. The app is not affiliated with any religious organization.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
